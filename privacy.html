Hoop Training Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the Hoop Training app ("the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is operated by Jaelyn Watson.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not visible to us.
  • Training preferences: your skill level, training goal, and daily time available, which we use to build your workouts.
  • Activity data: workouts you complete, so we can show your progress and streaks.
  • Subscription status: whether you have an active Premium subscription. Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To personalize your daily workouts and show your progress.
  • To unlock Premium content if you subscribe.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the app:

  • Supabase stores account and training data and handles sign-in.
  • RevenueCat helps manage subscriptions and verify Premium access.
  • Apple processes in-app purchases and subscriptions.

These providers process data only to provide their services to us.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time in the app: open Profile and tap Delete account. Subscriptions are managed through your Apple account, and deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. You can cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use industry-standard protections, including encrypted connections and database access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
